How to use WNDR

Presented by Chambers Creations, LLC

Get around

  • 🏙City chips (All, Dallas, Fort Worth, Austin) jump the map to that city and filter the list.
  • 🔎Search looks through names, neighborhoods, vibe notes, rates and booking notes.
  • ☰Filters: work spot type, free or paid podcast spaces, and outdoor seating only. “Reset all” clears them.
  • ◎Near me uses your phone's location, drops a blue dot and sorts both lists by distance.
  • ↕Bottom panel: swipe it up or down, or tap the handle, to switch between peek, half and full. Tap the map to tuck it away. Switch between the Work and Podcast tabs.
  • 📍Tap a pin or a list row for details: the vibe note and its source, hours, Wi-Fi notes, and big buttons for Directions (Google Maps), Call, Email and Website.

Layers (stack button, top right)

  • Work spots: calm cafés, bookstores, libraries, hotel lobbies and patios good for a laptop or a private talk.
  • Podcast spaces: a filled purple mic pin is free (mostly library studios); a white pin with a purple ring is paid.
  • Chill zones: a soft amber glow around each spot; the deeper the glow, the more good spots are clustered there.
  • City boundaries: dashed outlines of Dallas, Fort Worth, Austin and nearby cities from OpenStreetMap.
  • 5-mile rings: green rings show what's within 5 miles of a free studio; dashed amber rings do the same for paid studios.
  • Library branches: public and campus libraries from OpenStreetMap, not individually checked like the main spots.
  • 🗺Basemaps: OpenStreetMap (default), Calm (muted), Night (dimmed) and Humanitarian.

Keep it on your phone

In Chrome on Android, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. On iPhone Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. WNDR then opens like an app (it needs a connection for the map).

Call ahead. Hours and studio rates were checked from each place's site on Nov 12, 2026 and can change. Confirm by phone before you head out or book a session.
