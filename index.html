How to use WNDR

WNDR = Wi-Fi, Nooks, Drinks, Recording
Presented by Chambers Creations, LLC

Get around

  • 🏙Region chips scroll sideways: Dallas, Fort Worth, Austin, OKC, Edmond, Guthrie, Tupelo, Northern NM (Santa Fe, Taos, Red River and Angel Fire together), each NM town, San Diego, and All. Tap one to jump there; its spots load only then, so the app stays fast.
  • 🗺Pan or zoom into a region and its spots load automatically. Zoomed out, dark bubbles mark each region with its spot count; tap one to go there.
  • 🔎Search looks through names, neighborhoods, vibe notes, rates, booking notes and your own notes, across every region.
  • ☰Filters (the button at the end of the Work / Podcast / Saved row): work spot type, free or paid podcast spaces, and outdoor seating only. “Reset all” clears them.
  • ◎Near me uses your location, jumps to the nearest WNDR region and sorts the lists by distance. If you've already allowed location, WNDR opens on your nearest region; otherwise it opens on Dallas.
  • ↕Bottom panel: swipe it up or down, or tap the handle. Tabs: Work, Podcast and Saved.
  • 📍Tap a pin or a list row for details: the vibe note and its source, hours, Wi-Fi, and buttons for Call, Email and Website.

Directions

  • 🧭Every spot has Google Maps, Waze and Apple Maps buttons that start navigation to the spot's exact coordinates. Where a pin is only approximate, the buttons search by name and address instead.

Save spots and keep notes

  • ♥Heart a spot on its list row or details page to save it. The Saved tab lists your hearts and any spot you've written a note on.
  • ✎My note on each details page is private and saves as you type, e.g. “best table by the window” or “Wi-Fi drops after 3”. Notes show on the details page and in the list, and search finds them.
  • ⤴Share spot opens your phone's share menu (or copies a link) that opens WNDR right on that spot. Tick “Include my note” to add your note to the message.
  • ⇅Export / Import in the Saved tab saves your hearts and notes as a file, so you can back them up or move them to another phone or browser.
  • 🔒Everything stays on this device: there are no accounts and no public notes. Clearing your browser data clears them, so export now and then.

Layers (stack button, top right)

  • Work spots: calm cafés, bookstores, libraries, hotel lobbies and patios good for a laptop or a private talk.
  • Podcast spaces: a filled purple mic pin is free (mostly library studios); a white pin with a purple ring is paid.
  • Chill zones: a soft amber glow; the deeper the glow, the more good spots are clustered there.
  • City boundaries: dashed outlines from OpenStreetMap.
  • 5-mile rings: green around free studios; dashed amber around paid ones.
  • Library branches: public libraries from OpenStreetMap, not individually checked like the main spots.
  • 🗺Basemaps: OpenStreetMap (default), Calm (muted), Night (dimmed) and Humanitarian.

Keep it on your phone

In Chrome on Android, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. On iPhone Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. WNDR then opens like an app (it needs a connection for the map).

Call ahead. Hours and studio rates were checked from each place's site (DFW and Austin on Nov 12, 2026; the new cities on Nov 13, 2026) and can change. Confirm by phone before you head out or book a session.
